Verify in 5 minutes that EventOwl only reads your account

You can check in about five minutes that EventOwl only reads your account and that its numbers are right: turn Algo Trading off and see that the panel keeps working, compare its Reconcile box with your account balance, and recompute one trade from the audit CSV.

The three checks

  • Turn Algo Trading off. The panel still works, because it never sends orders.
  • Open the Overview tab: Reconcile compares the sum of all deals with your account balance.
  • Press Export CSV and recompute any number from its inputs in Excel or with your own tools.

1. Algo Trading off

Click the Algo Trading button in the MetaTrader 5 toolbar so that it is off. While it is off, the terminal refuses every order request from an Expert Advisor. Now use the panel: switch tabs, press Refresh, export a CSV. Everything keeps working, because the panel never sends orders.

You can also open the Journal tab of the Toolbox (Ctrl+T) while you use the panel. Every order request appears there, including refused ones. The panel leaves no order lines.

2. Reconcile

The Overview tab has a Reconcile box. It adds up every deal in the history: profit, swap, commission and fee of every trade, plus deposits and withdrawals. It compares the sum with your account balance. With the full history loaded, the difference is 0.00.

To compare with MetaTrader itself, open the History tab, choose "All history", and compare profit, commission and swap with the panel. The box also splits the sum into trades and other deals, so the two views line up.

3. Recompute one trade

Press Export CSV. The file is written to MQL5 > Files > EventOwl > export and opens in Excel. Each row is one trade with every input:

  • entry and exit time and price, volume, initial stop loss and where it came from;
  • 1R in money, profit, swap, commission, fee and net result;
  • MAE and MFE in price, money and R, and the lowest and highest price used;
  • spread at entry, slippage, matched events with their times, session and fix;
  • flags that mark approximate values.

Pick one trade and recompute:

  • net result = profit + swap + commission + fee;
  • R = net result / 1R;
  • MAE in R = MAE in money / 1R.

Settings you can leave off

In Tools > Options > Expert Advisors, "Allow DLL imports" can stay off and the WebRequest list can stay empty. Neither edition needs them: both use no DLL and no WebRequest. EventOwl uses no network. EventOwl Pro can send its alerts to your phone through MetaTrader's own push notifications; this is optional and off by default. The economic calendar both editions read is the one built into MetaTrader 5.

What else is checked before each release

A script scans the source code before every build and fails the build if it finds a trade function, WebRequest, a socket function or a DLL import. A second script checks that the store text, this site and the panel's About tab say the same things.

EventOwl and EventOwl Pro 1.00 for MetaTrader 5. A read-only panel. It never opens, changes or closes orders. It describes what already happened and does not forecast.
