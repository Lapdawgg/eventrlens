Guides for analysing your own MT5 trades

These guides show how to analyse your own MetaTrader 5 trades with standard MQL5 functions. Each one explains the method, gives a short code example and lists the pitfalls. You do not need any product to follow them.

  • How to measure MAE and MFE of closed MT5 trades: the largest move against and in favour of each trade, from M1 bars.
  • How to tag MT5 trades by economic calendar events: which CPI, NFP or FOMC release was near each entry or exit, with the daylight saving correction.
  • How to calculate the R multiple of MT5 trades: net result divided by the initial risk, from the stop loss on the entry deal.
