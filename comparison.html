Event R Lens compared with Strategy Ledger, EA Performance Hub, Magic Stats, Myfxbook and FX Blue

Event R Lens is the tool in this list that tags each trade with the economic event near its entry or exit and with the Tokyo or London fix. Several others do more in other areas: Strategy Ledger Pro has correlation, prop limits, an execution report and 11 languages; Myfxbook and FX Blue offer free public account pages. The table below was checked on 2026-11-18, and every row links to its source.

How to read the table

  • Yes: the feature is described on the product page.
  • Partly: a narrower version is described (the note says how).
  • Not listed: we did not find it on the page. That does not prove the product lacks it.
  • Pages change. If a row is out of date, please say so in the comments of the Event R Lens product page on MQL5.

Where other tools do more

  • Strategy Ledger Pro:
    • Correlation of daily results.
    • Prop limits (daily loss, maximum drawdown, profit target).
    • An execution report.
    • Strategy Tester import with a per-trade backtest vs. live comparison.
    • What-if and sizing tools.
    • Share cards and alerts.
    • 11 languages.
    • On sale since August 2026, with frequent updates and 22 reviews on 2026-11-18.
  • Strategy Ledger (free): Monte Carlo drawdown and a slippage estimate in a free tool.
  • Myfxbook and FX Blue: free public account pages that others can verify, several accounts in one portfolio, widgets and APIs, and MAE/MFE for many years.
  • Price: Strategy Ledger and Magic Stats are free, like Event R Lens.

Where Event R Lens differs

  • Event tags: Each trade carries the high-importance economic event near its entry or exit (for example USD CPI or USD NFP), matched by the calendar's event code and corrected for daylight saving. We did not find this per-trade tag in the products above.
  • Fix windows: Trades near the Tokyo or London fix are tagged. We did not find this in an analysis tool.
  • R, MAE and MFE together: R multiple, MAE and MFE are shown in price, money and R on the same row as the event, session and fix, with every input in the audit CSV. MAE and MFE alone are common: at least six MQL5 products show them.
  • Pro: results by event and by fix window, free-form combination queries with saved queries, and a lift table of labels among the biggest winners and losers.

Method

  • Date: All pages were read on 2026-11-18.
  • MQL5 Market: Products were found by web search and direct links, because the Market's own search could not be queried.
  • Myfxbook: Facts come from search result summaries only, because the site refused automated access.
  • Cell wording: Each cell summarises the page; it does not quote it.
