Event R Lens formulas: R multiple, MAE, MFE, event tags and statistics

Every number Event R Lens shows, and exactly how it is calculated, for engine version 1.00. The same definitions appear behind the i icons in the panel, and every input is in the audit CSV, so you can repeat any calculation. All times in the history are trade server time; the panel converts them to UTC with a server time model. Money is in the account currency.

0. Server time model

  • Default model "NY+7": server time = New York time + 7 h.
    • That is GMT+2 when the US is on standard time and GMT+3 during US daylight saving time.
    • US DST runs from the 2nd Sunday of March 07:00 UTC to the 1st Sunday of November 06:00 UTC.
    • This was verified for IC Markets, including the weeks when US and EU clocks differ.
  • Other models: fixed offset, or "EU DST" (base offset + 1 h from the last Sunday of March 01:00 UTC to the last Sunday of October 01:00 UTC).
  • Calendar times: The MT5 economic calendar returns event times shifted by the server's offset *at the moment of reading*. Event UTC = calendar time − current server offset. The current offset is round((TimeTradeServer() − TimeGMT()) / 30 min) and is used only when it matches the model.

1. Trades from deals

  • Grouping: Deals of type BUY or SELL are grouped by DEAL_POSITION_ID.
  • Direction: The type of the first entry deal sets the direction.
  • Entry price: Volume-weighted average of the entry deals.
  • Exit price: Volume-weighted average of the exit deals (OUT and OUT_BY).
  • Times: Entry time = first entry deal. Exit time = last exit deal.
  • Partial closes: Belong to one trade.
  • Netting reversal (INOUT):
    • The deal closes the open volume and opens the rest in the opposite direction. This becomes two trades: <id> and <id>-2.
    • The deal's profit goes to the closing part.
    • Its swap, commission and fee are split by volume.
  • Separate charge deals: Other deals that carry the same position id (e.g. a commission charged as a separate deal) are added to that trade.
  • Net result = profit + swap + commission + fee.
  • Open positions: Shown, but never counted in statistics.

2. Initial risk, 1R

  • SL: The SL of the first entry deal (DEAL_SL, which MT5 copies from the opening order).
    • MT5 does not store later SL changes.
    • The SL on the exit deal is the SL at the moment of closing, so it is not used.
  • 1R money = |loss| if the first entry volume had been closed at that SL. Formula: OrderCalcProfit(type, symbol, first volume, first entry price, SL).
    • The conversion uses current market rates. For USD-quoted symbols on a USD account (XAUUSD, USTEC, BTCUSD) it is exact. For crosses and JPY pairs it can differ slightly from the historical value.
    • If OrderCalcProfit fails, tick value × ticks is used and the trade carries the flag risk_from_tick_value.
    • A result of 0 for a non-zero move means MetaTrader has no quotes yet (for example right after the terminal starts). It is treated as unknown, never as 0. A trade with a stop loss but no priced 1R carries the flag risk_no_price_data. The panel waits up to 60 s for the terminal to connect before it calculates.
  • No SL on the entry deal:
    • If the panel recorded the SL later (forward journal), that SL is used (flag sl_from_journal).
    • Otherwise the user's fallback applies: a fixed money amount, or a % of the balance at entry. The trade is then marked r_estimate.
    • With no fallback the trade has no R.
  • Balance at entry = sum of all deal results (deposits included) before the entry moment.
  • R multiple = net result / 1R money.

3. MAE and MFE

  • Excursions (in price units, never negative):
    • LONG: MAE = entry − lowest price, MFE = highest price − entry.
    • SHORT: MAE = highest price − entry, MFE = entry − lowest price.
  • M1 mode (default):
    • Bars from the minute of entry to the minute of exit, both included.
    • LONG uses bid bars.
    • SHORT uses ask approximated as bid + the bar's spread (flag short_ask_from_bar_spread).
    • The first and last bars include prices from before entry and after exit, so M1 values are approximate (flag excursion_m1_approx).
  • Tick mode (optional): Every tick between entry and exit; LONG uses bid, SHORT uses ask. Falls back to M1 when ticks are missing or the trade is longer than 24 h.
  • Money: The price move applied to the total volume from the weighted entry price, through the same conversion as 1R.
  • In R: MAE money / 1R money.

4. Spread at entry

  • From ticks: ask − bid of the tick nearest to the entry moment, within 60 s. If two ticks are equally near, the earlier one wins.
  • Without ticks: The M1 bar's spread × point (flag spread_from_bar).
  • Two passes: The engine first fills every trade from M1 data (fast). A second pass then replaces spread, market-order slippage and, if enabled, MAE/MFE with tick-based values where ticks exist. It runs newest trades first, in the background, with progress shown. A tick request costs about 0.03–0.1 s in MT5. Until a trade is refined it keeps the M1-based value and its flag.

5. Slippage

  • Sign: Positive = worse for the trader. Buy: fill − reference. Sell: reference − fill.
  • Pending orders: The reference is the order price.
  • Market orders:
    • If the broker stored a requested price, that is the reference.
    • Otherwise the reference is the quote at the order's setup time (ask for buy, bid for sell; flag slippage_vs_quote). IC Markets stores 0 for market orders.

6. Event tags

  • Which events: High-importance calendar events whose time is in [moment − X, moment + Y] for the entry or the exit (default X = Y = 15 min).
  • Currencies: The symbol's fiat currencies: base and profit currency, if they are fiat. XAUUSD, USTEC and BTCUSD → USD. EURUSD → EUR and USD.
  • Matching: By the language-independent event code, not by the name; the name comes in the terminal language.
  • Label: Currency + short label, e.g. "USD NFP", "USD CPI", "USD FOMC".
  • Other events: A readable title made from the code, e.g. "USD EIA Crude Oil Stocks Change"; period suffixes (m/m, y/y, q/q) are dropped.
  • Same minute: Events in the same minute appear once per label. CPI m/m, y/y and core all become one "USD CPI".
  • Audit CSV: Records the event times in server time, the offset in minutes from the matched moment, and which side matched (entry, exit, or both).
  • Calendar unavailable: If the calendar does not answer (e.g. error 5401 on some networks), event tags are off and the panel says so.

7. Session

Label of the entry time, using local clocks with their own daylight saving rules:

SessionLocal hours
AsiaTokyo 09:00–18:00
LondonLondon 08:00–17:00
New YorkNew York 08:00–17:00
  • Precedence: London-NY overlap > London > New York > Asia > Off-hours.
  • Crypto weekend: Crypto symbols get "Weekend" from Friday 17:00 to Sunday 17:00 New York time.

8. Fix windows

  • Windows: Tokyo 09:55 JST and London 16:00 London time, ±10 min, on weekdays.
  • Tagging: A trade is tagged when its entry or exit falls inside the window. Holding through a fix is not tagged.

9. User tags

  • Sources:
    • A keyword found in the entry comment (case-insensitive). Pro input: "news, breakout"; the tag is the keyword.
    • A magic number. Pro input: "12345=Breakout, 777"; the tag is the name, or "magic:777".
    • Manual tags typed in the trade detail popup (Pro, account history only), stored in MQL5\Files\EventRLens\<server>_<login>_tags.txt when you press Enter. Comma separated; a "|" inside a tag becomes "/".
  • Duplicates: Removed.

10. Query statistics

  • Filters: symbol, direction, event, session, fix, tag, weekday and hour of entry (server time). Only closed trades are counted.
  • Event, fix and tag filters:
    • "any": no filter.
    • "*": any non-empty value.
    • "-": none.
    • Or a specific value.
  • Outcome: Win if R > 0.05, loss if R < −0.05, else breakeven. Without R, the sign of the net result is used.
  • Hit rate = wins / N.
  • Mean R, median R: Over trades with R. Expected R = mean R.
  • 95% CI of mean R: Percentile bootstrap, 2,000 resamples, fixed seed, so it is reproducible.
  • Max MAE: Largest MAE in R and in money.
  • Longest losing streak: Consecutive losses by exit time; a breakeven ends the streak.
  • Total net: Sum of net results.
  • Estimated R: Trades with an estimated 1R are counted and shown separately. They can be excluded.
  • Sample warnings:
    • N < 30: "small sample".
    • Fewer than 10 trades with R: no mean, median or CI; the raw list only.

11. Reconcile

  • Balance check: Sum of every deal's profit + swap + commission + fee (deposits included) vs. ACCOUNT_BALANCE. With the full history selected the difference must be 0.00.
  • Split: The sum is split into trades and other deals (deposits, withdrawals, credits, unattached charges). This lets it be compared with the MT5 History tab.

12. Pro: Big R finder

  • Trades used: Closed trades with a usable R (R estimates can be excluded).
  • Group: The top (or bottom) 10% or 20% of these trades by R, at least 1 trade: group size = ceil(fraction x N).
  • Threshold: The R of the last trade in the group ("R at least" for winners, "R at most" for losers).
  • Labels per trade: Event, session, fix, symbol, direction, weekday (server time of entry), hour of entry, user tag. A trade with two events or tags counts once for each.
  • Share in group = trades of the group with the label / group size.
  • Share of all = trades with the label / N.
  • Lift = share in group / share of all. Lift above 1: the label is more common among the biggest winners (or losers) than in general.
  • Listing: Labels with fewer than 2 trades in the group are not listed. Labels on every trade (lift always 1) are not listed. Sorted by lift, then by count in the group. Labels with fewer than 5 trades in the group are shown in yellow: their lift is unreliable.
  • It describes past trades; it does not say why.

13. Pro: Replay and reshuffles

  • R sequence: The R values of the selected closed trades, ordered by exit time.
  • Replay: Start at 100. After each trade: equity x (1 + risk% x R). If equity reaches 0, it stays 0 ("ruin").
  • Max drawdown: The largest fall from a running peak, in % of that peak.
  • Longest losing streak: Most consecutive trades with R below -0.05 (the breakeven band).
  • Reshuffles: 10,000 random orders of the same R values (Fisher-Yates shuffle, xorshift64* with a fixed seed, so the numbers repeat). For each order: max drawdown and longest losing streak.
  • Percentiles: Value at position ceil(p x 10,000) of the sorted results (median, 90%, 95%, 99%).
  • Limit: Share of orders whose max drawdown reached the chosen limit. "Equity reached 0": share of orders with ruin.
  • Title on screen: "Replay of your past trades. Not a forecast." Reshuffles reuse your own R values; they do not model future trades.

14. Pro: Prop news rule audit

  • Rule (entered by the user): N minutes before and M minutes after a news event; applies to the open and/or the close of a trade; a winning trade in the window has its profit removed, or counted at X%.
  • Events: High-importance events of the MT5 economic calendar for the symbol's currencies (same mapping as section 6), times corrected to UTC with the server time model.
  • In the window: event - N min <= trade moment <= event + M min, checked for the open and/or the close.
  • Shown per trade: the nearest matching event, its server time, "trade - event" in minutes (negative = before the news), and the side (open, close, open+close).
  • Profit at risk: For a trade in the window with net > 0: net (rule "removed"), or net x (1 - X/100) (rule "counts at X%"). Losing trades are listed but not changed.
  • Net after the rule = total net of all closed trades - profit at risk.
  • This is not a database of firm rules. Firms may use other news sources and times.

15. Pro: Strategy Tester report import

  • File: HTML report saved from the Strategy Tester, copied to MQL5\Files\EventRLens\import\. UTF-16 and UTF-8 are read.
  • Deals table: time, deal, symbol, type, direction (in, out, in/out, out by), volume, price, order, commission, swap, profit, balance, comment.
  • Orders table: the stop loss of each order. The SL of the order that opened a position is its initial SL (1R).
  • Position ids: The report has none. Positions are matched first in, first out per symbol; an exit deal that closes several positions is split by volume (profit, commission and swap in proportion).
  • Reconcile: The sum of all deals (deposit included) vs. the report's final balance; the report's totals row is also checked.
  • Every imported trade carries the flag simulated_tester_report. The simulated set is never mixed with the account history. No tick refinement.

16. Pro: share card

  • PNG, 1200 x 630, of the current query: N, wins/losses/breakeven, hit rate, mean R with its 95% range, median R, max MAE in R (with how many trades have MAE data), longest losing streak, a histogram of R in 0.5R buckets from -3R to +5R (values outside go to the end buckets), and the date range.
  • No account number, broker or money amounts. Footer: "N = ... closed trades. Past results, not a forecast." A simulated set is marked "SIMULATED".
  • Written as PNG by the panel itself; no external library or service is used.

Known limitations

  • M1 approximation: MAE/MFE from M1 is approximate (edge bars; SHORT ask from bar spread).
  • M1 depth: The terminal keeps M1 bars only back to a limit (Tools > Options > Charts > Max bars in chart; 100,000 bars is about 4 to 5 months). Older trades show no MAE/MFE and are counted as "no price data". After 3 empty requests in a row for a symbol, older trades of that symbol are not requested again until Refresh.
  • Tick depth: Tick data at IC Markets reaches about 6 months back for USTEC/BTCUSD.
  • Conversion rates: 1R and excursion money use current conversion rates for non-USD-quoted symbols.
  • Unrecorded SLs: An SL set after entry is unknown unless the panel was running.
  • Calendar network: The economic calendar may not be reachable on some networks; event tags are then off.
  • Strategy Tester: The calendar is not available in the Strategy Tester.
