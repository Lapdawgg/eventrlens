Event R Lens FAQ

Short answers to the questions people ask most about Event R Lens, a read-only MetaTrader 5 panel that breaks down your own closed trades by economic event, session and fix window.

Does Event R Lens place trades?

No. It never opens, changes or closes orders or positions. The source code contains no trade functions, and a script fails the build if one appears. You can check this yourself: turn Algo Trading off and the panel keeps working.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. It uses no network functions, no WebRequest and no DLL. Everything stays in your terminal. It writes files only to MQL5 > Files > EventRLens: your display settings, a small journal of stop losses of positions opened while the panel runs, and the files you ask for, such as CSV exports.

Does it work with Algo Trading turned off?

Yes. Algo Trading only controls whether programs may send orders. The panel reads the history, draws, and exports with Algo Trading off.

How is the R multiple calculated?

R = net result / 1R. The net result is profit + swap + commission + fee. 1R is the loss you would have had if the first entry had been closed at its initial stop loss, the stop loss on the entry deal. MetaTrader calculates that amount with OrderCalcProfit. Details are on the Formulas page.

What if my trades have no stop loss?

Then there is no initial risk to divide by, and the trade has no R. If the panel was running when you set a stop loss later, it recorded that stop loss and uses it. Otherwise you can set a rule in the inputs: a fixed money amount or a percent of the balance as 1R. Those trades are marked "R estimate" and can be left out of every statistic.

How are MAE and MFE calculated?

MAE (maximum adverse excursion) is the largest move against the trade between entry and exit; MFE is the largest move in its favour. The panel takes the M1 bars from the minute of entry to the minute of exit. For a long trade, MAE = entry minus the lowest price and MFE = the highest price minus entry; for a short trade the other way round. The values are shown in price, money and R. M1 values are approximate because the first and last bar include prices from before entry and after exit, and the panel marks them so.

Why does it need M1 history, and why do some trades show no MAE?

MAE and MFE need the price path between entry and exit, and the M1 bars are that path. MetaTrader keeps M1 bars only back to a limit set in Tools > Options > Charts > Max bars in chart. Trades older than that limit show no MAE and MFE, and the Overview counts them as "no price data". A larger setting gives more history.

How does it know which economic event a trade was near?

It reads the economic calendar built into MetaTrader 5. A trade gets an event tag when a high-importance event of the symbol's currencies falls within 15 minutes before or after its entry or its exit. You can change both windows. Gold, US indices and Bitcoin use US events; currency pairs use both currencies.

Why are calendar times corrected for daylight saving?

The MetaTrader 5 calendar returns event times shifted by the broker server's offset at the moment you read it, not at the time of the event. Without a correction, events from the other half of the year would be one hour off. The panel converts every time to UTC with a broker time model; the default is New York time + 7 hours, which many brokers use.

Does it work with prop firm accounts?

The panel only reads the account, so it works on any MetaTrader 5 account, including prop firm accounts. Some firms have rules about running Expert Advisors at all; check your firm's terms. Event R Lens sends no orders.

Can it check my trades against my prop firm's rules?

Event R Lens Pro can, with the numbers you enter from your firm's current terms:

  • daily loss limit;
  • maximum loss (static or trailing);
  • news window (minutes before and after high-importance news, for the open and/or the close);
  • consistency (the top day, your largest daily profit, as a share of total profit);
  • risk per trade;
  • no weekend holding.

The Prop Guard tab lists the past trades and days that broke a rule. It also shows live how much room is left today, when the next news window starts for an open position, and how long until the weekend. It is not a database of firm rules: firms measure in different ways and change their terms, so check yours. The panel warns; it never closes positions.

Can it warn me before a news release?

Event R Lens Pro has an Event Radar: the high-importance events of the next 8 days for the chart symbol and your open positions, each with your own past trades near that event (count, hit rate, mean R). It can show a terminal alert some minutes before an event, before your news window starts for an open position, at 80% of your daily loss limit, and before the weekend. The alerts are local terminal alerts; the panel sends nothing over the network.

What is the rule simulator?

It answers "what if I had followed this rule?" on your own closed trades. Examples: skip entries within 15 minutes of news, stop for the day after 2 losses, move the stop to entry at +1R, close half at +2R. It shows the change in hit rate, mean R, total R, drawdown and losing streak, and whether the effect holds in both halves of your history. It is tested on the same past trades, so it is not a forecast.

Does it work in the Strategy Tester?

The Strategy Tester has no account history, so the panel shows "No account history in Strategy Tester. Attach to a live or demo chart." It opens without errors. The economic calendar is not available in the Strategy Tester either.

Can I analyse a Strategy Tester report?

Event R Lens Pro can import a Strategy Tester HTML report. The imported trades are marked simulated and are never mixed with your account history. Reconcile compares them with the report's final balance. Because the report has no position ids, positions are matched first in, first out per symbol.

What does Event R Lens Pro add?

Event R Lens shows every trade with its R, MAE, MFE and the event and fix window near it, and groups results by session, symbol and direction. Event R Lens Pro groups them by economic event and by fix window as well, so you see your hit rate and mean R around CPI, NFP or the Tokyo fix. It adds the Event Radar, Prop Guard, the rule simulator, the combination query, the Big R finder, the replay, Strategy Tester report import, your own tags, share cards, and spread and slippage from ticks for recent trades. The full list is in the table on the home page.

Does it forecast results?

No. Every number describes trades that already closed. The Pro replay shows how your own past R values would have added up at another risk, and 10,000 reshuffles show how much their order mattered. Its title on screen is "Replay of your past trades. Not a forecast."

How small a sample is too small?

Below 30 trades the panel shows a yellow "small sample" note. Below 10 trades with R, it hides the mean, the median and the range, and shows only the list of trades.

Does it work with netting and hedging accounts?

Yes. Trades are built from deals by position id. Partial closes stay in one trade, and a netting reversal becomes two trades.

Which symbols does it support?

Any symbol in your history: currency pairs, metals, indices, crypto and others. Event tags need a currency the calendar knows. Crypto symbols get a Weekend session label from Friday 17:00 to Sunday 17:00 New York time.

Why does the economic calendar not load?

On some networks the terminal cannot reach the calendar service. The panel then says so and turns event tags off; everything else works. It tries once per session, because an unanswered request blocks the terminal for about 50 seconds; press Refresh to try again. If the Calendar tab of the terminal is empty too, the network is the cause.

Is there a MetaTrader 4 version?

No. Event R Lens is for MetaTrader 5 only.

How do I check its numbers?

Turn Algo Trading off, compare Reconcile with your balance, and recompute a trade from the audit CSV. The steps are on Verify in 5 minutes.
