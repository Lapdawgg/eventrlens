EventOwl FAQ

Short answers to the questions people ask most about EventOwl, a read-only MetaTrader 5 panel that breaks down your own closed trades by economic event, session and fix window.

Which MetaTrader 5 tool shows how my trades did around NFP and CPI?

EventOwl marks every closed trade with the major release near it (NFP, CPI, rate decisions, GDP, PCE, retail sales and jobs data, and Fed chair appearances) and shows its R, MAE and MFE. EventOwl Pro adds results by event: hit rate, mean R with its 95% range, max MAE and net result near each event type.

How do I see the MAE and MFE of my closed trades in MT5?

EventOwl, the free edition, shows the MAE and MFE of every closed trade in price, money and R, from M1 bars. The trade detail window and the audit CSV show every input, so you can check each value.

How did gold or the Nasdaq move after past CPI releases?

The News reaction tab of EventOwl Pro lists every past major release of the chart symbol's currencies from the last 400 days, with the move 5, 15 and 60 minutes later and the range of the first 15 minutes, split by better or worse than expected. These are past reactions, not a forecast.

What does EventOwl cost?

EventOwl: Free. EventOwl Pro: 59 USD. Both run inside MetaTrader 5 and come through the MQL5 Market.

Does EventOwl place trades?

No. It never opens, changes or closes orders or positions. The source code contains no trade functions, and a script fails the build if one appears. You can check this yourself: turn Algo Trading off and the panel keeps working.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. It uses no WebRequest, no sockets and no DLL. Everything stays in your terminal. The one exception is optional and off by default: EventOwl Pro can send its alerts to your phone through MetaTrader's own push notifications, if you turn that on and enter your MetaQuotes ID in the terminal. It writes files only to MQL5 > Files > EventOwl: your display settings, a small journal of stop losses of positions opened while the panel runs, and the files you ask for, such as CSV exports. EventOwl Pro also saves your firm preset choice there.

Does it work with Algo Trading turned off?

Yes. Algo Trading only controls whether programs may send orders. The panel reads the history, draws, and exports with Algo Trading off.

How is the R multiple calculated?

R = net result / 1R. The net result is profit + swap + commission + fee. 1R is the loss you would have had if the first entry had been closed at its initial stop loss, the stop loss on the entry deal. MetaTrader calculates that amount with OrderCalcProfit. Details are on the Formulas page.

What if my trades have no stop loss?

Then there is no initial risk to divide by, and the trade has no R. If the panel was running when you set a stop loss later, it recorded that stop loss and uses it. Otherwise you can set a rule in the inputs: a fixed money amount or a percent of the balance as 1R. Those trades are marked "R estimate", and the Overview counts them under Data quality. In EventOwl Pro, the query and the Big R finder can leave them out.

How are MAE and MFE calculated?

MAE (maximum adverse excursion) is the largest move against the trade between entry and exit; MFE is the largest move in its favour. The panel takes the M1 bars from the minute of entry to the minute of exit. For a long trade, MAE = entry minus the lowest price and MFE = the highest price minus entry; for a short trade the other way round. The values are shown in price, money and R. M1 values are approximate because the first and last bar include prices from before entry and after exit, and the panel marks them so.

Why does it need M1 history, and why do some trades show no MAE?

MAE and MFE need the price path between entry and exit, and the M1 bars are that path. MetaTrader keeps M1 bars only back to a limit set in Tools > Options > Charts > Max bars in chart. Trades older than that limit show no MAE and MFE, and the Overview counts them as "no price data". A larger setting gives more history.

How does it know which economic event a trade was near?

It reads the economic calendar built into MetaTrader 5. A trade gets an event tag when a major event of the symbol's currencies falls within 15 minutes before or after its entry or its exit. Major means a central bank rate decision or press conference, nonfarm payrolls, CPI, GDP, PCE, retail sales, a jobs report (employment change, unemployment rate, hourly earnings) or a Fed chair appearance; an input switches to every event the calendar marks high-importance. You can change both windows. Gold, US indices and Bitcoin use US events; currency pairs use both currencies.

Why are calendar times corrected for daylight saving?

The MetaTrader 5 calendar returns event times shifted by the broker server's offset at the moment you read it, not at the time of the event. Without a correction, events from the other half of the year would be one hour off. The panel converts every time to UTC with a broker time model; the default is New York time + 7 hours, which many brokers use.

Does it work with prop firm accounts?

The panel only reads the account, so it works on any MetaTrader 5 account, including prop firm accounts. Some firms have rules about running Expert Advisors at all; check your firm's terms. EventOwl sends no orders.

Can it check my trades against my prop firm's rules?

EventOwl Pro can. Pick your firm, program and stage from the built-in presets (FTMO, FundedNext, The5ers, FundingPips, Alpha Capital Group, E8 Markets, Blueberry Funded and Audacity Capital, read from each firm's site on 2026-11-22), or enter the numbers yourself. Prop Guard checks the rules on your past trades and live; it warns and never closes positions.

The rules you can enter yourself:

  • daily loss limit;
  • maximum loss (static or trailing);
  • news window (minutes before and after high-importance news, for the open and/or the close);
  • consistency (the top day, your largest daily profit, as a share of total profit);
  • risk per trade;
  • no weekend holding.

Minimum holding time (short-trade rules) has no input: it comes from the presets only. Consistency and minimum holding time are checked on your history; the other rules on your history and live.

The Prop Guard tab lists the past trades and days that broke a rule. It also shows live how much room is left today and to the maximum loss floor, the risk of your open positions, when the next news window starts for an open position, and how long until the weekend. Each preset shows its source and date, and lists where the firm measures differently from the panel, for example a daily limit taken from the higher of balance and equity, or a trailing floor that stops at the initial balance. Firms change their terms, sometimes by purchase date, so check yours.

Can it warn me before a news release?

EventOwl Pro has an Event Radar: the major events of the next 8 days (an input switches to every high-importance event) for the chart symbol and your open positions, each with your own past trades near that event (count, hit rate, mean R). It can show a terminal alert some minutes before an event, before your news window starts for an open position, at 80% of your daily loss limit, and 30 minutes before the FX week closes on Friday at 17:00 New York time if your rules do not allow weekend holding. If you turn on the input "Also send the alerts to your phone" (off by default), MetaTrader also sends them to your phone with its own push notifications. EventOwl, the free edition, has no alerts and uses no network.

What is the rule simulator?

It answers "what if I had followed this rule?" on your own closed trades. Examples: skip entries within 15 minutes of news, stop for the day after 2 losses, move the stop to entry at +1R, close half at +2R. It shows the change in hit rate, mean R, total R, drawdown and losing streak, and whether the effect holds in both halves of your history. It is tested on the same past trades, so it is not a forecast.

Does it work in the Strategy Tester?

The Strategy Tester has no account history, so the panel shows "No account history in Strategy Tester. Attach to a live or demo chart." It opens without errors. The economic calendar is not available in the Strategy Tester either.

Can I analyse a Strategy Tester report?

EventOwl Pro can import a Strategy Tester HTML report. The imported trades are marked simulated and are never mixed with your account history. Reconcile compares them with the report's final balance. Because the report has no position ids, positions are matched first in, first out per symbol.

What does EventOwl Pro add?

EventOwl shows every trade with its R, MAE, MFE and the event and fix window near it, and groups results by session, symbol and direction. EventOwl Pro groups them by economic event and by fix window as well, so you see your hit rate and mean R around CPI, NFP or the Tokyo fix. It adds the Event Radar, News reaction (how your chart symbol moved after each past major release), Prop Guard, the rule simulator, the combination query, the Big R finder, the replay, Strategy Tester report import, your own tags, share cards, and spread and slippage from ticks for recent trades. The full list is in the table on the home page.

Does it tell me to buy or sell?

No. It does not give trade signals. EventOwl Pro shows how your chart symbol moved 5, 15 and 60 minutes after each past major release, and whether one direction was common (for example "mostly down, 9 of 12"). That describes past reactions; the next release can move the other way.

Does it forecast results?

No. EventOwl describes trades that already closed; EventOwl Pro also shows how the market moved after past releases and the current account state. None of it is a forecast. The Pro replay shows how your own past R values would have added up at another risk, and 10,000 reshuffles show how much their order mattered. Its title on screen is "Replay of your past trades. Not a forecast."

How small a sample is too small?

Below 30 trades the panel shows a yellow "small sample" note. Below 10 trades with R, it hides the mean, the median and the range, and shows only the list of trades.

Does it work with netting and hedging accounts?

Yes. Trades are built from deals by position id. Partial closes stay in one trade, and a netting reversal becomes two trades.

Which symbols does it support?

Any symbol in your history: currency pairs, metals, indices, crypto and others. Event tags need a currency the calendar knows. Crypto symbols get a Weekend session label from Friday 17:00 to Sunday 17:00 New York time.

Why does the economic calendar not load?

On some networks the terminal cannot reach the calendar service. The panel then says so and turns event tags off; everything else works. It tries once per session, because an unanswered request blocks the terminal for about 50 seconds; press Refresh to try again. If the Calendar tab of the terminal is empty too, the network is the cause.

How do I contact the developer?

Questions, ideas and bug reports: open an issue on GitHub, or send the author a private message on MQL5.com. Bug reports help most with your MT5 build and a screenshot of the panel's About tab. Open an issue on GitHub.

Is there a MetaTrader 4 version?

Not now. EventOwl is for MetaTrader 5. A MetaTrader 4 version is on the roadmap, open for votes.

How do I check its numbers?

Turn Algo Trading off, compare Reconcile with your balance, and recompute a trade from the audit CSV. The steps are on Verify in 5 minutes.
